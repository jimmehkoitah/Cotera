Questions you might have

The short answers.

Do we have to move our data?

No. Cotera works on top of the warehouse you already have.

What exactly is Coco?

Our AI agent. Anything a person can do in the app, Coco can do in plain language.

Is it only for technical people?

No. The app and Coco are built for GTM teams. Engineers come in for custom work.

How fast can we build something?

An audience, a report or a workflow is same-day. Deep custom work is an engineering project.

Is it enterprise-ready?

Single sign-on, role-based permissions, and it runs on Google Cloud.

Are the automations reliable?

Long-running workflows resume safely after a restart, so jobs don't silently drop.

For the technical crowd

Under the hood.

1 · Your warehouse

Your data stays put

Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres or DuckDB — Cotera never copies it out.

2 · ERA (query engine)

Write the logic once

ERA turns it into each warehouse's own SQL and runs it inside the warehouse.

3 · Models and workflows

Automations that don't drop

Long workflows are journaled (Restate), so they resume safely after a restart.

4 · Integrations

Pushes into your GTM tools

Connectors for Klaviyo, Braze, Shopify, Slack and more, plus webhooks.

5 · App and Coco

Coco is an AI agent, not a chatbot

It does what a person can in the app, in plain language — and isn't tied to one model vendor.

Underneath: SSO + permissions (WorkOS), usage billing (Orb), on Google Cloud, multi-tenant and isolated
